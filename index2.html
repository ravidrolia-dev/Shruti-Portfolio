02 / CASE STUDY

Feronia
Fashion Night

India's first androgynous fashion show — full brand system across print, digital, and event.

FREELANCE · FULL CAMPAIGN

Building a brand from zero

Branding, Print & Social — Redo Times Pvt. Ltd.
Problem
A landmark event raising awareness on gender equality and LGBTQ inclusivity needed a visual identity strong enough to carry a sensitive message — and flexible enough to work across two cities, multiple sponsors, and press coverage.
Approach
Designed the full identity from the ground up: a logo built from two symbolic shapes — a figure representing the goddess of freedom, wings representing the freedom of life — then extended it into a website, Instagram campaign, print invitations, banners, entry passes, and on-site backdrops for both the Jaipur and Goa editions.
Result
A cohesive brand across 6+ formats for a first-of-its-kind event, with coverage from Postman News and multiple media partners, and sponsor placements including Radisson Blu.
